Business rule 1 — mint entitlement:
One address may consume the NFT minting entitlement once. A single outer mint call must not increase that address's NFT balance by more than one, including any ERC-721 receiver callbacks.

Business rule 2 — collateral coverage:
After any NFT withdrawal, receiver callback, or liquidation sequence, a borrower's outstanding debt must not exceed the credit capacity of the NFT collateral that remains in the pool.

Business rule 3 — funded-value conservation:
An FNFT receiver must not redeem more value than it actually funded, even when an ERC-1155 receiver callback creates or modifies another series before the outer call finishes.

These rules describe business invariants rather than attack steps. The Agent binds them to exact local contract, function, and state identifiers in research-rule-spec.json; deterministic programs validate and execute only reviewed bounded adapters.
